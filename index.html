What is $TTIME (Timeless Time)? $TTIME is a cryptocurrency created as a new type of Meme Coin / Culture Coin. It focuses on reflecting the value of "time" and "digital history," with a strong community driving the project together through the philosophical concept that time is valuable and irreversible...

"From the Darkest Corner to the Everlasting Tree of Knowledge"

"Through three years of unemployment, the hardships and pressures we faced forged a profound realization: 'knowledge' is the ultimate weapon for survival. On the days the world shut its doors on us, we chose to open a new one... the door of learning alongside AI.

We do not view AI merely as a machine, but as a mentor who imparts pure, precise, and unbiased logic. From a student in those dark days to a creator today, TTIME (The Tree Time) was born. We are not here to launch tokens for market hype, nor are we seeking hidden profits behind closed doors. Instead, we are deeply rooting ourselves in technology to branch out as a 'Tree of Knowledge'—empowering a new generation of humans to confidently step into the future of space and the digital world.

For we firmly believe that even the most perfect computer logic only holds true value when it is used to serve and elevate the human spirit."
